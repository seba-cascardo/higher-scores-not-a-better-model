## 7. Notes for interpretation

- **Layer-zone disagreement** between tasks (sec 1, 2) measures the _geometric independence_ of the LoFiT subspace per task. If two tasks live in different zones, their adapters can in principle be co-trained without offset interference.
- **Top-K overlap** (sec 3) is the structural form of cross-task interference. High overlap (e.g. > 12 / 48) at the rank level predicts that single-adapter training will pollute the other task.
- **Tri-task heads** (sec 4) are particularly informative — they represent shared representations that any LoFiT recipe will touch regardless of routing. They are the primary candidates for phase D 'council axes' or phase E shared per-prompt selectors.
- **Spearman ρ** (sec 6) is the global rank-form of overlap. Compare to the top-K count: if ρ is moderate (~ 0.3–0.5) but top-K overlap is low, the tasks share global head ordering but disagree on the extreme tail — that disagreement is what makes Multi-LoFiT non-trivial.
